Gone are the days when it took days together to shop for a dress material, visit the tailor and get that divine stitching done. Major fashion boutiques across the city now bridge the gaps of tailoring and offer you the best of services under one roof. Not only do they house a varied range of clothing, but also take care of stitching your un-stitched suits with in-house tailors making them a one-stop shop for all your clothing needs. Here is a list of the ultimate fashion destinations in the city.

## **Kim’s Exclusive**

**About the Boutique:** If you are a passionate female, crazy about the latest trends in clothing, then Kim’s Exclusive is your ultimate fashion destination. Popular for its exquisite collections, the boutique houses the newest and freshest readymade garments meant for today’s modern women. You can get the perfect blend of branded clothing, party-wear and regular dress materials all assorted in this single shop. For someone who loves to sport class in her looks worrying the least about the price tags, you will find the best collection in town that suits your taste. Catch the hottest of trends all up for grab at this leading designer dress store of Bhubaneswar.

**Locate the shop:** Kim’s Exclusive is located at 261, Bapuji Nagar, Bhubaneswar – 751009. Contact No – 9861023925

## **Masterjee Boutique**

**About the Boutique:** So you have picked a gorgeous dress material and are wondering where to get it stitched. Masterjee Boutique at Janpath is the answer to all your stitching needs. It has been a pioneer in providing tailoring services over the recent years. Known for its elegant work and timely deliveries, you can keep your dressmaking worries at bay by opting for its stitching services. Bring the best of cut, neck designs and stitches on to your dress by customising the outfit as per your own style and taste. The master craftsmen at Masterjee know how to make one as per your taste. Rest assured you get your money’s worth in return for the classy stitching and that perfect fit.

**Locate the Shop :** Masterjee Boutique is located on Plot No.622 - A, Janpath. Contact No. - 0674 - 6532235

## Utsav

**About the Boutique:** Utsav comes as a one stop shop for all sorts of Indian women clothing. It houses a huge collection of bridal sarees, trendy Salwar Kameez and other Indian ladies wear like Lehenga Cholis etc. Popular for stocking the best Indian designer wear and also regular Indian women’s clothing, the shop is frequented by the youth who prefer the latest trends over traditional attire. You can now dress up in ethnic wear but would still look gorgeous thanks to the many shades and designs available at Utasv. From a wedding saree to an Indo-Western outfit, the boutique has it all across different price points to suit your pocket. Fashion lovers can now shop till they drop to explore the many options that await them at Utsav.

**Locate the Shop:** The boutique is located at 124, Janpath, Ashok Nagar, Near Panda Enterprises. Contact No: 0674 – 2531343

## **Bhatia Fashion**

**About the Boutique:** A popular store for the regular shoppers is the Bhatia Fashions outlet in the Forum Mart Mall. For someone who wants the best dress materials but is also concerned about budget, this store houses a variety of trendy and traditional ladies wear at amazing prices. If you are a shopalcoholic, then Bhatia Fashions is your ideal destination to get the freshest of fashion as often as you want. The shop also houses some of the best fragrances in the mall. So get your clothing at the best available prices and walk away with a cool perfume to add that extra zing to your attire!

**Locate the Shop:** The boutique is located in Shop No 102 of Forum Mart, Kharavela Nagar
