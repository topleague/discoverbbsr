Stone carving in Odisha is a traditional craft and has been practiced for centuries and reached dizzy heights of excellence due to disciplined efforts of generations.

The huge repertoire of stone carving is best revealed in the architecture and rock-cut sculptures of Lingraja, Jagannath, Mukteshwara and other temples of Odisha.
