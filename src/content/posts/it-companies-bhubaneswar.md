Bhubaneswar is slowly turning out to be the favored destination of many IT & ITES companies as many big players have already setup their operation at Bhubaneswar. The IT sector scenario in Bhubaneswar finally seems to be changing for the better. This trend is surely going to benefit the young engineering graduates of the city who had no other option but to leave the state and move to other IT hubs like Bangaluru, Hyderabad & Pune in search of jobs and better carrier opportunities. These young professionals can finally hope to fulfill their dream of working in world class companies in their own state. Infosys was the first big player that started its operation in Bhubaneswar way back in 1997. This particular move virtually kick started the expansion of IT sector in Bhubaneswar which has the potential to become a world class IT hub. So which are the best IT companies to work for in Bhubaneswar? Let’s take a look.

## **Infosys**

Infosys began its operations in Bhubaneswar way back in 1997 when Bhubaneswar was slowly gaining currency as a potential IT hub in the East. The Bhubaneswar campus is spread on an area of approximately 10 acres and can easily accommodate 4,018 employees. The facilities that are available to the employees of the Bhubaneswar campus are at par with other Infosys campuses across India and the company is housed inside an ultramodern building at Infocity. In terms of facilities, the campus offers all the modern facilities like library facilities, super markets, cafes, food courts and gym etc. Infosys has been a regular at various engineering college campuses across the twin city and you can opt for your preferred location if you join Infosys.

**Location:** Infosys Bhubaneswar campus is located at Plot No. E / 4, Info City, Bhubaneswar - 751 024, Odisha

## **TATA Consultancy Services (TCS)**

The global IT giant TCS opened up its Bhubaneswar campus TCS Kalinga Park in January 2009. TCS Kalinga Park, which is being developed over an area of 45 acres of land, is the latest addition to the company’s unique Global Network Delivery Model (GNDM). The first unit of the facility with a capacity of 1,000 seats was made operational in the first phase. Upon completion, the facility will have a total capacity of 7,000 seats. TCS Kalinga Park is amongst the greenest and the most energy efficient buildings in India. TCS Kalinga Park has been designed as India's first LEED (Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design) rated IT Campus conforming to the highest LEED certification rating - Platinum. The campus is an approved Special Economic Zone and will serve global TCS customers in various industry segments. TCS has record for recruiting in huge numbers from various reputed engineering colleges in Odisha and with the addition of this new facility at Bhubaneswar the young engineer students of the city will not have look for options outside Odisha.

**Location:** Tata Consultancy Services, Kalinga Park, IT/ITES Special Economic Zone, Plot - 35,Chandaka Industrial Estate, Patia, Chandrasekharpur, Bhubaneswar - 751 024, Odisha

## **Wipro Technologies**

Wipro Technologies didn’t have a fully operational development centre at Bhubaneswar but its BPO vertical has been operational in Bhubaneswar for the past few years. The BPO has staff strength of over 500 employees and the company has a full fledged fully operational campus at Infocity and the BPO has an office at Saheed Nagar. The company has plans to add new staff to its existing strength for its Voice and Non-voice processes. Engineering graduates looking to get into IT and ITES can choose Wipro to jumpstart their career in Bhubaneswar.

**Location:** Wipro Technology, Infocity, Chandrasekharpur, Bhubaneswar

**Wipro BPO:** A/86, 3rd Floor, Saheed Nagar, Bhubaneswar – 751007, Odisha

**Mahindra Satyam**

Mahindra Satyam has a fully operational development centre at Bhubaneswar and is operational since the past few years. Mahindra Satyam has been recruiting aggressively from various campuses in the city like ITER, CV Raman and others. The company offers attractive package to the new recruits and the Bhubaneswar centre gets many important projects. People who join the Mahindra Satyam Bhubaneswar can hope to work on the very best of technology as the will be working under the Mahindra Satyam Development Centre.

**Location:** Satyam Computer Services Limited, Mahindra Satyam Development Center, Plot No. S-1, Maitree Vihar Road, Chandrasekharpur, Bhubaneswar - 751 023, Odisha

## **Mindfire Solutions**

Although not a part of the big league, Mindfire Solutions can very well be called one of India’s leading providers of Software & IT services to more than 100 clients in US, Europe, Australia and Asia in the 10 years of its operation. The company has over 700 people, spread across 2 Advanced Development Centers (ADCs) in India, one based out of Mathura Road, New Delhi and the other based out of Tower-2000, Mancheswar Industrial Estate. The office is equipped with ultra modern facilities and the company acquired the fourth floor space of the Tower 2000. This floor has a space of 10,000 sq ft and can accommodate 250+ workstations bring the total capacity of Mindfire’s Bhubaneswar operation to about 550. The company is a Microsoft Gold Certified partner & an Apple Developer Connection Premier Member. It has been in operation in Bhubaneswar since the past 10 years and has been recruiting many young IT professional for its Advanced Development Centre at Bhubaneswar.

**Location:** Mindfire Solutions, 2nd, 3rd & 4th Floors, Tower-2000, Mancheswar Industrial Estate, Bhubaneswar – 751010, Odisha

## **Discoverture Solutions**

Discoverture Solutions, a Scottsdale, Arizona (USA)-based IT services firm has been operational with the inception its state-of-the art development centre in Infocity, Bhubaneswar since April, 2011. It is an Insurance and Healthcare industry focused global information technology solutions and service provider has with offices in the UK and India. It caters to the service needs of the company’s North America and Europian clients. From a humble yet ambitious start (25-employees at OCAC Building) in 2003, Discoverture Solutions's Bhubaneswar campus has taken a giant stride and now prides on it being the only development center in India, with space for up to 500 associates.

**Location:** Plot No. E/48, Infocity Chandrasekharpur Bhubaneswar - 751024, Odisha
