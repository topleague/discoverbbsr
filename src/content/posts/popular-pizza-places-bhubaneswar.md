## Pizza Hut

**About the Outlet:** As the name suggests, the shop is your favourite destination to the best pizzas in the town. It has become the neighbourhood cafeteria of the youth, serving to delight with its amazing ambience and making every visit a memorable and cherished experience. If you are planning for a date or a hangout with friends, those endless conversations can never be complete without some salivating food and relaxing drinks. This outlet which is amongst the very first pizza parlours of Bhubaneswar houses much more than just your fresh baked pizza. You get a whole array of freshly sautéed pastas & delicious appetizers to mocktails, desserts, soups and salads to feast on. Located in Forum Mart, the shop is regularly thronged by pizza-lovers in huge numbers.

**Locate the Shop:** The shop is housed in Forum Mart, Kharvel Nagar and you can reach it by following the easy directions.

## Domino’s Pizza

**About the Outlet:** The newest sensation in the league of pizzerias is Domino’s Pizza that’s recreating the ever increasing demand for pizza amongst the youth all over again. The venture has gained huge popularity amongst its loyal customers because of its ability to deliver delicious Pizzas that are superior in quality at an exceptionally fast pace. In a short span of time, it has established a reputation for being a home-delivery specialist, capable of delivering pizzas within 30 minutes or else free to a community of its loyal customers across the city. For the ones looking for affordable and value for money options, you can try out the Fun Meal and Pizza Mania varieties available at the store. A fresh favourite of the populace, Domino’s seems to be committed to bringing fun, happiness and convenience to its consumers by delivering delicious pizzas at their doorsteps.

**Locate the Shop:** The pizzeria is on the ground floor Shop No- 6 at the Xavier Crossing, Jaydev Vihar that’s opposite the Odissi Research Centre.

## Smokin’ Joes

**About the Outlet:** One of the oldest and most frequented places for pizza lovers in the town is Smokin’ Joes. The place gave that all-needed boost to the image of the city, establishing itself as a proper pizza corner much to the delight of family-men and youth. The shop has withstood multinational and local competition over the years mainly because of its superb quality of food and its excellent service. You can easily order your steaming hot pizza over a phone call. Their online order service hasn’t yet started in the city. Known for its fresh bread and delicious spread of vegetarian and non-vegetarian toppings, it serves a combination that’s sure to appeal the city’s populace.

**Locate the Shop:** The shop is located at Plot No. P1, P1/A, XIM Square, Jay Dev Vihar. Follow the directions to reach the place with ease.

## Cafe Coffee Day- The Lounge

**About the Oulet:** Popular among the city-folks for its brewing hot coffee, not many know the fact that the place offers some very good pizza to munch along while the beverage of your choice. What makes the place so special is that you can customise your own pizza with your choice of fillings, toppings and bread! Apart from the menu that tells you what CCD wants you to eat; at The Lounge you get to choose your own pizza and the same is baked keeping your taste and choice in consideration. So if you want to grab that delicious hand tossed pizza loaded with your favourite toppings anytime, you know where to drop in!

**Locate the Shop:** You can find a CCD in different locations across the length and breadth of the city but the most popular ones are the ones opposite Ram Mandir at the New Marrion Complex, the one at XIMB Square & the ones near Gopabandhu Square and Barmunda.
