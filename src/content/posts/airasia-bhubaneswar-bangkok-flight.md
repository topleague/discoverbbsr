Air Asia will launch direct flight between Bhubanswar and Bangkok thrice a week starting from December 7 next.

The Air Asia plane will fly to Bangkok on every Wednesday, Friday and Sunday starting from December 7 next at a promotional fare of Rs 2999 per trip.

Anyone who books ticket between October 10 – 21 next for travel from December 7, 2018 to – March 30, 2019 can avail the benefit.

![AirAsia Promotional Price](/images/airasia-bhubaneswar-bangkok-flight/Screenshot-2018-10-11-06.45.46-1024x352.png)

Air Asia India Head of Marketing Rajkumar Paranthaman, said that Air Asia plans to penetrate the Indian market in the latter half of 2018 as it is a major market with great growth potential, especially as Bangkok and other Thai cities have proven popular destinations for Indians.

\[caption id="attachment\_6058" align="aligncenter" width="449"\]![Take Me to Grand Palace ](/images/airasia-bhubaneswar-bangkok-flight/Take-Me-to-Grand-Palace--449x680.jpeg) Take Me to Grand Palace\[/caption\]

Air Asia passengers can easily make a transit from Don Mueang International Airport to domestic destinations in Thailand that include Phuket, Krabi, Chiang Mai and Chiang Rai as well as international destinations such as Singapore, Bali, Ho Chi Minh City, Hong Kong and Macau.
