What wisdom can you find that is greater than kindness? - **Jean Jacques Rousseau**

Welcome to Bhubaneswar, the City of Temples – a city that boasts of a million population dwelling in accommodations ranging from slums to luxurious apartments.

There are also fellow citizens that don’t have a home to call their own.

They live out there on the streets deprived of their family in their old age, for reasons unknown.

So what if life has rendered them destitute in the Lord’s Abode! They have a heart large enough to hold kindness that could melt you heart and make you fall in love their random act of kindness.

The crew led by Raj Sampad from Incendiary Films met one such person whose story should move you to tears.

But despite faced with life’s tragedy, the homeless has found a way of living – sharing his food with the stray animals and birds.

## **Introducing – Kamala Lochan Baliarsingh**

Indeed, stories like this inspire us to be more compassionate and appreciate life as it comes to us.

Because if he can smile, we must!
