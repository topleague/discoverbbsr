A search on Google says that a lot of tourists and travelers to the city look for budget hotels in Bhubaneswar. While TripAdvisor is a nice place to find the reviews of various hotel customers, we still thought we needed to come up with our very own Bhubaneswar budget hotel reviews to help tourists looking for budget hotels within a specific price range. Blame it on the soaring real estate prices of the city, finding budget hotels in Bhubaneswar is often a daunting task. Last month, one of our community members wrote to us expressing his concerns about the lack of information on the budget hotels in the city. While the definition of a budget hotel is subject to personal interpretation, many tourists look for budget hotels at lower cost with standard adherence to hygiene, services and safety. So, we decided to put together a list of five hotels based on the following criteria:

\-> Tariff (Starting Price = Rs.1600 or less)

\-> Customers Reviews (Bhubaneswar Hotels TripAdvisor)

**Research Methodology**

We prepared the list of hotels by visiting various online travel portals and reading reviews and ratings of the customers who have shared their experience at the hotels. We also took into account the findablity i.e., level of online presence of the hotels in terms of doing a Google search. Other vital factors such as online hotel booking facilities have also been considered to decide the final list.

Let’s take a look at the budget hotels in Bhubaneswar.

## #1 – Hotel Upasana

This hotel is located in the Old Station area, this hotel on Cuttack Puri Road is just about 3 kilometers away from the airport. Guests can choose to stay in any of the 20 well-furnished rooms equipped with a satellite television. Indian and international cuisines can be ordered through room service, which is available between 7a.m and 10p.m. Other guest amenities include: taxi service, doctor-on-call, guide service, laundry and housekeeping, and parking.

**Location**: Plot No 2, Old Station Square, Netaji Subhash Marg, Upasana Old Station Square.

**Reviews**: Read reviews [about their services](http://www.tripadvisor.in/Hotel_Review-g297661-d1178694-Reviews-Upasana_Hotel-Bhubaneswar_Odisha.html) on  Tripadvisor.

**Website**: www.hotelupasana.com/

**Lowest Tariff**: Rs.750 (2 Rooms, 3 Bedded)

**Phone**: +91-674-231 0044

## #2 – Hotel Vennus Inn

Conveniently located from both Railway station and Airport. The hotel is fairly popular and close to many popular temples in the city. It’s also close to many electronics shops in Bapuji Nagar.

**Location**: 217, Bapuji Nagar, Bhubaneswar

**Reviews**: Read reviews about their services on Tripadvisor.

**Website**: www.venusinngroup.com/venus\_inn

**Lowest Tariff**: Rs.750 (Single Room Deluxe)

**Phone**: +91- 0674-2597908

## #3 – Hotel Priya

The hotel is at walk-able distance from Odisha Secretariat and Odisha Vidhan Sabha. All other Government offices are within a radius of 2 km from the Hotel Venus Inn, Shopping malls and market places are within close reach. It is very conveniently located to reach Railway station, Bus stand as well as Airport.

**Location**: A-30/1, Kharavela Nagar, UNIT-3

**Reviews**: Read reviews about their services on Tripadvisor.

## #4 – Hotel Kharavela

As per their website, the hotel Kharavela is a unit of Shikha Resorts Pvt. Ltd. Contemporary and internationally styled interiors, infused with local decorative elements and modern Indian art define the interiors at the hotel. Located in the city’s greenest precinct, it is within walking distance of the Khandagiri hills.
