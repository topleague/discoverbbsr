Bank Probationary Officers Exam (Bank PO) is conducted to select personnel for prestigious and high profile jobs in the banking industry. With growing competition in the banking industry, many aspiring bank professionals look for professional coaching to optimize their success rate for various competitive exams conducted by the public sector banks around the year. The career security and social repute attached with the job of a bank manager are perceived as the prime driving force  behind people looking for professional banking coaching classes in the city. This article is designed to help people find some of the popular names imparting coaching to the banking job aspirants in the city.

## **ARIFIN Academy**

**About the Institute:** ARIFIN was started in the year 1989 as the first premium coaching center in Odisha. It is presently engaged in giving coaching to aspirants of Bank POs and other competitive exams and provides necessary proficiency in job skill, group discussion and personality development. With an increasing number of vacancies in banking sector and a proportionate rise in the number of aspirants, ARIFIN Academy has stood the test of time by providing quality coaching to the students and has got a huge success rate. The institute is popular for its unique teaching techniques and valuable course materials. Its highly qualified faculties simplify all the doubts and confusion of students. It also provides complete development of theoretical and practical skills of students, which is very helpful to increase the self-confidence of the students.

**Located At:** The institute is located at Acharya Vihar Square

## **TIMES School of Banking**

**About the Institute:** With competition taking new leaps and bounds every other day, the number of students enrolling themselves to this pioneer institute is huge. A leader among the banking coaching centers, TIMES School of Banking believes in giving the best to its students in terms of education and expertise. The center focuses on training students with concepts and conditioning them for the exam by giving them the previous years’ question papers along with study materials to practice. The out-of-the-box questions make the tests unique and hone the skills of the students. Studying the pattern in general, and considering the nature of questions asked in exam, they frame tests with likely questions and provide the best methods to answer them.

**Located At:** The institute is located at Saheed Nagar.

## **IMS**

**About the Institute:** IMS is a reputed coaching center having 34 years of experience in imparting quality training programs to aspirants. They offer coaching to aspirants of MBA, Bank PO and Bank Clerical exams. An excellent set of faculties and mentors motivate and shape the attitude of the aspirants so that they take the right decisions regarding their career. The institute provides individual attention to each student by qualified and experienced teaching faculties. Apart from regular classes and mock tests, it also provides doubt-clearing sessions and previous year question paper discussions to empower the students. Over the years, the institute has proved its ability to nurture a lot of students to get through competitive entrance examinations.

**Located At:** The institute is located at two places - N B Towers, Nayapalli and Master Canteen Square.

## **T.I.M.E** 

**About the Institute:** One of the leading coaching institutes with a pan-India presence. In keeping with its tradition of always maintaining a watchful eye on market trends and tapping them, T.I.M.E. has introduced training programmes for almost all competitive exams including Bank PO & Clerical exams and its results have been head and shoulders above the rest in the league. The main drivers of this success are T.I.M.E.’s commitment to students, its pan-India presence, its highly committed team of R&D professionals and its well-researched study material and unique pedagogy. T.I.M.E. trains thousands of students every year across the country in aptitude tests and communication skills as a part of preparing them for the tedious banking exams and helps them grow into successful banking professionals.

**Located At:**  There are two centers of the institute, one at Nayapalli and the other at B-43, Sahid Nagar.

## **Mahendra's Institute**

**About the Institute:** The institute is considered as a perfect coaching center by candidates for cracking Bank PO exams. Famous for its excellent lectures along with comprehensive study materials, Mahendra’s provides students with practice model test papers for revision even at home. This coaching center is an intermediate path for aspirants to get into a bright career. The institute believes in improve the quality of students appearing for the banking exams by imparting them with high quality education for banking entrance exams, providing training and development services and disseminating knowledge through books, journals and magazines meant for Bank PO exams. With a commitment and focus to offer world-class program which is designed to help each student realize his or her full potential, the institute is fully dedicated to produce some outstanding results in the competitive exams every year.

**Located At:** The institute is located in the 3rd Floor, Mahaveer Complex at CRP Square.
