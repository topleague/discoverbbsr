Owing to its vibrant culture, natural splendour, and advancing infrastructures, Odisha receives nearly 65,000 foreign tourists every year. Bhubaneswar, the capital city, is the preferred destination for most of the tourists for their stay. The capital city boasts of many luxurious as well as budget hotel accommodation facilities that are ready to receive and serve their guests round the clock any day. This post is designed to help tourists looking for luxurious and premium hotel accommodations having state of the art facilities. Here's a list of the best 8 premium hotels in Bhubaneswar, tariff and other facilities for you to consider for your stay in the temple city.

## **Trident**

**Hotel Overview:** Trident Hilton Bhubaneswar is a First Class International Hotel. It is spaciously situated amidst fourteen acres of lush green space and fruit orchards with a kilometre long jogging track for winding through its beautiful lush green gardens. It is the most premium hotel in Odisha and has the best amenities in town. The hotel provides wireless internet access in all guest rooms and public areas. The newly renovated rooms now are fully refurbished with all modern facilities and amenities and offer scenic views of the landscaped surroundings. The interiors of the hotel are deeply inspired by the traditional temple architecture of Odisha.

Among other things, the hotel offers services like concierge, photocopying service, and facsimile, currency exchange, car rental and exclusive car parking for its guests. The hotel has wheel chairs for the sick and the physically challenged guests. There are special rooms with customised fixtures for the physically challenged guests. Trident has a very good business centre and is the preferred venue for meetings and conferences by corporates.

**Rating**: 5 Star

**Hotel in Bhubaneswar Tariff (Approx)**: Rs. 8,000

**How to Reach:** The Hotel is located at CB-1, Nayapalli, Bhubaneswar - 751 013 and is about 7 km drive away from the airport.

## **Hotel Hindustan International (HHI)**

**Hotel Overview:** HHI Bhubaneswar is the first boutique hotel in the city. It is part of the prestigious Hindustan International group of hotels The HHI Bhubaneswar is the perfect destination for travellers who come to Odisha for business or for pleasure. The hotel has one of the best rom sizes in its class and they are very spacious as they vary in between 350 to 400 sq. ft.

Amenities include four fixture luxurious toilet in all rooms with glass partition between rooms and toilet with remote control curtains, Plasma TV & DVD in every room, well stocked mini bar in every room, 24hrs room service facility, complimentary pickup from the airport, 6 state-of-the-art suites and 1 presidential suite with i-pod and wi-fi connectivity, wooden flooring with throw in rugs, five conference halls to suit every corporate function and Business Center facility.

The exclusive restaurants and the lounge Bar are a hit among the city’s elite. The HHI has a very spacious banquet hall - Crystal – 9000 sq. ft. state of the art ball room for marriage and social functions. Staying at the HHI is synonymous to announcing your status symbol in class and style.

**Rating**: 4 Star

**Hotel in Bhubaneswar Tariff (Approx)**: Rs. 4,500

**How to Reach:** Situated downtown on Janpath at Kharvel Nagar, it is within 1km radius from the Railway Station and 3 kms away from the airport. Just follow the given directions to reach this hotel.

## **Mayfair Lagoon**

**Hotel Overview:** An elegant low rise hotel spread on 10 acres of nature in its splendid glory. The resort’s 1.5 acre artificial Lagoon and beautifully landscaped gardens are the organs of a complex water body, beautifully holding everything together. All the water bodies within the Mayfair Lagoon are beautifully maintained. Located in the heart of Bhubaneswar and set amidst acres of lush green plants and foliage, Mayfair Lagoon is a short walk from the best of the shopping and entertainment centres that you can come across in the city. Its unparalleled style, elegance and proximity to nature along with its warm hospitality makes a stay at Mayfair Lagoon tranquil and truly memorable. Its intimate courtyards, fountains and exquisite works of art make it is a paradise for you to explore and discover the joys of living in the lap of luxury.

The luxury hotel boosts of excellent room service, business service and the best entertainment and leisure facility in the city. Mayfair  Lagoon has four multi cuisine restaurant, a bakery and pizzeria, a South Indian speciality restaurant and has a well stocked bar as well as a cool discotheque.

**Rating**: 5 Star

**Hotel in Bhubaneswar Tariff (Approx)**: Unknown

**How to Reach:** Conveniently located from the airport at Jayadev Vihar it is a 7 km drive away from the airport.

## **Swosti Premium**

**Hotel Overview:** For those who prefer class while spending more can opt for Hotel Swosti Premium which is a 4-star property, spread over 3 and a half acres of open space. Highly recommended by its guests, the hotel offers luxurious facilities with 4 dining options which includes Confucius – the authentic Chinese restaurant, Chandan – the restaurant specialising in Odia delicacies, a 24-hour coffee shop, a disco, a well stocked bar, luxurious swimming pool and conference facilities as well, at a competitive price of Rs. 4,500 per day. Hotel Swosti Plaza has been a part of Odisha's premier hotel chain – The Swosti Group of Hotels ever since its inception in 1984. Redefining the mantra of hospitality, The Swosti Group is dedicated to providing only the best in terms of amenities and comfort. This dedication towards providing the very best for its guests has brought with it numerous awards and laurels paving the way for the Swosti Group to achieve the highest turnover amongst hotel groups in Orissa.

Hotel Swosti Premium is surely going to make for beautiful memories for you and your family to cherish for a lifetime during your stay.

**Rating**: 3 Star

**Hotel in Bhubaneswar Tariff (Approx)**: Rs. 5,000

**How to Reach:**  The hotel is located at Jayadev Vihar, Bhubaneswar and is located near Nalco Bhawan. It is approximately 4 kms from Bhubaneswar Railway Station and 7 kms from Biju Patnaik Airport.

## **The New Marrion**

**Hotel Overview:** The New Marrion is a luxury hotel built in 1990. The hotel is the ultimate choice of vacationers and tourists. The excellent service and modern amenities have captivated the interests of many travellers, seeking absolute comfort. The New Marrion offers three different varieties of rooms - executive rooms, deluxe rooms and suite rooms depending on your budget and craving for luxury. All the rooms are equipped with all the modern amenities and the hotel offers the best services and all the facilities to its guests so that they enjoy a comfortable stay.

The perfect venue for any event – be it parties, meetings or weeding ceremony, Emerald, Sapphire and Ruby are the meeting and conference areas at The New Marrion equipped with state of art facilities like video conferencing, LCD projector, sound and light systems. The hotel has four multi cuisine restaurants, two coffee shops, two well stocked bars, a very good South Indian restaurants as well as a speciality sweet shop.

**Rating**: 3 Star

**Hotel in Bhubaneswar Tariff (Approx)**: Rs. Unknown

**How to Reach:** The hotel is situated only 5 kms away from the airport and is only a kilometre away from the railway station.

## **The Crown**

**Hotel Overview:** The Crown is amongst the best 3 Star Hotels is synonymous with style and elegance. The experience that one gets at the Crown is second to none. The hotel has customer care on its mind and goes that extra mile to make the guests feel at home. The hotel boasts of sixty eight beautifully designed luxurious rooms, four banquet halls, business center, theme based Indian cuisine restaurant, a specialty Chinese restaurant, bar, board room, health club, swimming pool and an exclusive coffee shop. Additional facilities like a poolside deck, the wide lawn area, the grand courtyard, and an open-air partying make it one of the most sought after hotel’s by foreign as well as domestic travellers. It is positioned in the city centre and tourists have easy access to the major tourist attractions of Bhubaneswar. This perfect positioning of the accommodation makes it easy to reach and connect to the city landscapes.

## **Ginger**

**Hotel Overview:** Ginger Bhubaneshwar provides all the special features needed by the modern business traveller and is assured of getting relaxing rooms equipped with business facilities at moderate rates. It is known for its Smart basics such as Smart Space rooms that are uniquely designed for providing utmost comfort, Safe Zone locker service for your valuables, gymnasium, well equipped meeting room with wi-fi and net connectivity, self check-in kiosk and is geared to make your stay comfortable and pleasant. Known as the best budget hotel in the city, its City Guide section helps you gain an insight into important locations in the city, its heritage, businesses and business centres and other key locations to visit.

**Rating**: 3 Star

**Hotel in Bhubaneswar Tariff (Approx)**: Rs. 3,000

**How to Reach:** It is located opposite Nalco Headquarters, Jaydev Vihar and is a 20 mins drive from the airport and is 15 minutes away from the railway station. You can follow the directions to reach Ginger easily.
